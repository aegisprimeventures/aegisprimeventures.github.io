About Us

Aegis Prime Ventures is building a modern, reliable, and scalable agribusiness in Nigeria. We specialize in:

- Poultry – Quality day-old chicks, broilers, layers, and related products  
- Livestock – Cattle, goats, sheep and other livestock supply  
- Agro Supply – Feeds, vaccines, equipment, and farm inputs  

Our mission is to support farmers and agro-entrepreneurs with consistent quality, fair pricing, and dependable supply chains.
